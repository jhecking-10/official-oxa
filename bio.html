ABØUT US

Ociexah (stylized and abbreviated as ØXA) is a group of near lifelong friends who have bonded over their shared dream of creating music together. Its members are Devon "Cove" Burns (lead vocals and bass), brothers Clayton (drums) and Blake Benford (guitars), and James Hecking (guitars). The project was initially formed in South Georgia in 2016, with the current lineup solidifying around 2018. The band's debut album is currently being actively written.
